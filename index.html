FAQ

Common Questions

A few things you may want to know before getting started.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on the number of pages, features, content and integrations. Contact us with your requirements and we'll share a clear quote before work begins.

How long does it take to build a website?

A simple business website may take around one to two weeks once the content and requirements are ready. Larger projects can take longer.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Websites are planned to be responsive and tested across common screen sizes.

Can you help with domain and hosting?

Yes. We can guide you through domain registration, hosting setup, DNS configuration and launch. Domain and hosting charges are quoted separately.

Can I update the website later?

Yes. We can agree on a suitable update process or ongoing maintenance plan depending on your website platform and needs.
