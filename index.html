After Project Delivery

Clear Scope. No Compulsory Monthly Management.

We focus on building and delivering your project. Ongoing website operations remain with your business.

What is included in our approach

Development, agreed feature testing, deployment to your domain and project handover. If a technical issue related to the delivered work appears, you can contact us for troubleshooting and a resolution plan.

What is not part of standard delivery

Daily website management, routine content or product updates, order processing, hosting administration and continuous monitoring. New changes, ongoing maintenance or extended support can be discussed and quoted separately.
